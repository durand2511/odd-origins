Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Odd Origins ("we") runs the YouTube channel @oddoriginstoons and an internal publishing tool (the "Odd Origins uploader") that uses the YouTube API Services.

What the uploader does

The uploader is used only by the channel owner. It uploads videos that we produced ourselves, together with their title, description and tags, to our own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and no other person signs in to it.

Data we access

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope youtube.upload) the uploader can upload videos to the owner's channel. It does not read, collect, store or share any data about viewers, other users or other channels. The OAuth token is stored only on our own private server and is used only for these uploads.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the uploader's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

Instagram and TikTok

The same uploader also posts our own videos to our own Instagram account (@oddoriginstoons, Instagram API with Instagram Login: instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish) and our own TikTok account (@oddoriginstoons, TikTok Content Posting API: user.info.basic, video.publish). It only reads the basic account details needed to post (account id, name and allowed privacy settings) and never accesses data of viewers or other accounts. Tokens are stored only on our own private server. The owner can revoke access at any time in the Instagram or TikTok app settings (apps and websites).

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.
